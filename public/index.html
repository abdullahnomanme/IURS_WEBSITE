About IURS

Welcome to Islamic University
Research Society


Our mission at Islamic University Research Society is to empower students through research, innovation, academic collaboration, and transformative learning experiences that inspire future researchers and youth leaders across Bangladesh and beyond.

Our vision is to establish a strong research culture among students and help Islamic University, Kushtia become one of the leading research-oriented universities in Bangladesh through the continuous development of young, capable researchers.

IURS is guided by the core values of Research Excellence & Innovation, Academic Integrity, Youth Leadership Development, Knowledge Sharing & Creativity, and International Collaboration across all disciplines.
